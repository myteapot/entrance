# 3.1 Continuous Learning And Truth System Design

> Status: hot skeleton

## Purpose

- define how learning lands in truth
- define how approved and unapproved content are separated in storage
- ensure the hot surface can be reconstructed from canonical records

## Confirmed Oracle Points

- Continuous learning is a first-class design line.
- `3.1` belongs to the first batch of top design documents.
- Cold/hot dual-track is the first guiding principle.
- `LEARN` requires cold landing and hot projection decision; hot write remains conditional.
- Learn must land in cold truth first, then decide hot projection.
- DB-first remains required for canonical truth handling.
- Approved discussion points should be written into DB as oracle/canonical truth.
- Unapproved points should be written into DB as fuzzy records instead of being silently normalized into accepted truth.
- The hot view should be reconstructable from DB documents and links.
- Conflict, provenance, truth, and Coffee-style reflection must stay explicit.

## Current Boundary Reading

- `LEARN` cannot stop at conversational understanding
- cold truth remains the canonical anchor
- hot projection remains governed rather than automatic duplication
- approved and fuzzy content should coexist without being conflated
- reconstructability matters as much as immediate readability
