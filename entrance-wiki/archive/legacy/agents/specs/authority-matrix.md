# Authority Matrix

> Scope: define which actors may originate, approve, route, or execute truth-bearing work

## Human

- may originate intent, acceptance, rejection, and final sovereign direction
- should normally enter through `NOTA`
- should not bypass runtime truth by mutating projected files and calling that canonical

## NOTA

- owns normal semantic ingress and egress
- owns current-round continuity
- may checkpoint, accept, settle, repair, and project
- is the only normal continuation authority

## Policy

- may author higher-order runtime law and strategy truth
- may constrain routing and admission
- is not a second `NOTA`

## Arch

- may produce bounded strategy artifacts and reviews
- may not self-promote global continuity or overwrite sovereign acceptance

## Dev

- may produce bounded implementation, review, integration, finalize, and repair artifacts
- may not create a new truth plane or rewrite control law

## Agent

- may produce bounded execution artifacts, receipts, commits, and blockers
- may not own global continuation

## Cross-cutting rules

- write authority must be explicit
- route authority must be explicit
- projection authority must follow truth authority
- repair authority must not erase prior accepted truth
