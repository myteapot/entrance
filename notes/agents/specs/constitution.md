# V0 Constitution

> Scope: freeze the top-level constitutional law for the post-consolidation Entrance runtime

## Constitutional identity

- Entrance is a local-first, DB-first runtime rather than a file-first app.
- The owner root is `~/.entrance`.
- The canonical runtime DB is `~/.entrance/data/entrance.db`.
- `README(Oracle)`, `Hot Root`, `Cold Docs`, GUI, CLI, and MCP are all projections from DB truth.

## Truth law

- DB is the only canonical semantic writer.
- Files may be regenerated from DB truth.
- Cold docs remain canonicalized in DB even when projected back into the repo.
- Recovery sources are import feeds, not peer truth planes.

## Control law

- Human is the final sovereign.
- `NOTA` is the only normal semantic ingress and egress.
- `Policy` is the highest internal writer.
- `Arch / Dev / Agent` are bounded execution lanes rather than peer continuation authorities.
- Cross-slot effect must travel through runtime-owned objects and receipts.

## Round law

- A `human round` is the primary continuity unit.
- Every meaningful human interaction should belong to one current round.
- A round must be checkpointable, acceptable, and settleable.
- `passed human round = acceptance`.
- `fully settled round` is stricter than acceptance and requires no outstanding next-step or projection debt.

## Projection law

- Projection always follows truth write.
- Projection freshness, dirty state, and repair are themselves truth concerns.
- Hot root remains fixed at six files.
- Oracle remains a route projection rather than a peer truth plane.

## Anti-Zeno law

- anti-Zeno is first-class.
- anti-Zeno is not a second truth plane.
- anti-Zeno must constrain recursion, repair cycling, and projection drift.
- Budget exhaustion must force repair, bounded closure, or human decision.

## Non-goals

- do not restore file-first authoring
- do not reopen multi-entry continuation
- do not expand product feature scope before runtime law is sharp
- do not create shadow truth through indexes, retrieval, or cached projections
